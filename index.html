Meet Maverick & Professor Bucktooth

Join Maverick, a regular guy, and his rather... *unique* companion, Professor Bucktooth, on their wild adventures. Professor Bucktooth, a plush beaver doll with a surprisingly profound (and chaotic) understanding of 'structural integrity' and 'romantic justice', often finds himself and Maverick in the most peculiar situations. From fairgrounds gone wild to impromptu Shakespearean performances and sticky syrup-fueled escapades, their story is one of unexpected friendship and delightful mayhem.

"It's Romantic Justice, Maverick! The most durable structure of all!" - Professor Bucktooth

"Professor, you just handcuffed a robber to a waitress. That's not how justice works." - Maverick

Professor Bucktooth: The Beaver of Beaver Logic

Professor Bucktooth isn't just any plush doll. He's a self-proclaimed expert in 'structural integrity', 'romantic justice', and general mayhem. With his squeaky voice and polyester vessel, he approaches every situation with an unwavering, albeit bizarre, logic. He believes in ensuring that all romantic gestures have proper scaffolding and that justice, when served, should be as durable as a beaver lodge.

Maverick: The Patient Partner

Maverick is the steadfast human companion to Professor Bucktooth. While often bewildered and sometimes exasperated by the Professor's antics, Maverick consistently finds himself pulled into the whirlwind of adventures. His calm demeanor and occasional witty remarks ground the Professor's wild schemes, making them an unlikely but inseparable duo.
